Nirnaya IQ supports light, dark, and system themes. Your choice is stored per-device — switching themes on your laptop won't affect what you see on your phone.

## Change the theme

1. Click your avatar in the bottom left of the sidebar.
2. Hover **Theme**.
3. Pick one of:
   - **Light** — always light, regardless of your OS.
   - **Dark** — always dark.
   - **System** — follow your operating system. Switches automatically when your OS toggles between day and night.

The change is instant. Reload isn't needed.

## Why a flash of white when I open the app?

The very first paint shows the default theme before the saved choice loads. We minimize this with a small preload script, but on a slow connection you may see a flash. Once the app boots, the choice sticks.

## Reduce motion

Dark mode is purely visual — it doesn't affect animations. If you also want to reduce motion (transitions, slides), set **Reduce Motion** in your operating system; Nirnaya IQ respects the system setting and disables non-essential animations automatically.

## High-contrast / accessibility

Both themes meet WCAG AA contrast on every component. If you'd like AAA-level contrast or a high-contrast variant, please email `support@companybrain.app` — we're tracking interest and would love to hear your use case.
