Invites are managed from **Settings → Team**. Only admins can invite or remove members.

## Send an invite

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. Click **Invite member** in the top right.
3. Enter the teammate's work email.
4. Choose a role:
   - **Admin** — can manage documents, invite users, see analytics, and configure the workspace.
   - **Member** — can chat, upload documents, and use templates.
5. Click **Send invite**.

They'll receive an email with a one-time link to join your workspace. Invites expire after **7 days**. If the link expires, you can resend it from the same screen.

## Resend or revoke an invite

Pending invites show up in the team list with a **Pending** badge. From the **⋯** menu you can:

- **Resend** — issues a new 7-day link, invalidating the old one.
- **Revoke** — cancels the invite immediately.

## Change a member's role

In the team list, click the role on a member's row and pick a new one. The change applies immediately — the next time they reload, their permissions reflect the new role.

## Remove a member

From the **⋯** menu next to a member, click **Remove**. Their account is detached from the workspace; their chat history stays for the record but they lose access to all documents.

## Limits

The number of seats included in your plan is shown at the top of **Settings → Team**. If you try to invite past your limit you'll see an **Upgrade required** message — click it to switch to a plan with more seats.
